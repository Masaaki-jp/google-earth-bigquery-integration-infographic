The Geospatial Data Landscape

Google Earth Engine provides an unprecedented catalog of planetary data. Understanding the composition of this catalog is the first step in leveraging it. This section breaks down the types of datasets now immediately queryable within BigQuery, revealing a strong foundation in optical and radar telemetry.

📸 Optical Imagery

Makes up the vast majority of the catalog. Includes Landsat and Sentinel-2 data, crucial for monitoring vegetation health, deforestation, and urban sprawl over time.

📡 Radar (SAR)

Synthetic Aperture Radar can see through clouds and operate at night. Essential for continuous monitoring of supply chain routes, flooding, and agricultural structures.

🌤️ Climate & Weather

Historical reanalysis and predictive models used to cross-reference enterprise logistics with extreme weather events and long-term climate risk.
